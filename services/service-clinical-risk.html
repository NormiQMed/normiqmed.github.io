ISO 14971 · CLINICAL EVALUATION

Clinical evidence & risk management.

Clinical evaluation and ISO 14971 risk management structured as a connected evidence chain — so risk files and clinical evidence support each other instead of being built in isolation.

What this covers

  • Clinical Evaluation Report (CER) development and structure
  • Benefit-risk framework construction aligned to intended use
  • ISO 14971 risk management file design and integration with design controls
  • Risk-to-evidence traceability across the product lifecycle
  • Literature review and clinical data strategy for evidence gaps

When this is typically needed

  • Building a CER for first EU MDR submission or periodic update
  • Risk management files exist but lack traceability to clinical evidence
  • Preparing benefit-risk documentation for Notified Body review
  • Expanding intended use or indications requiring updated clinical evidence

Applicable standards

ISO 14971 EU MDR 2017/745 MEDDEV 2.7/1
